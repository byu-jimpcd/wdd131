Cusco: Heart of the Incas

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Cusco: Heart of the Incas

This name represents Cusco as a historic city located in the Andes of Peru. It reflects the city's rich history, culture, traditions, and importance as a destination for visitors.

Logo: A stylized representation of mountains of Cusco

Site Purpose

The website provides information about Cusco, Peru, including its history, culture, tourist attractions, gastronomy, and useful information for visitors. The goal is to help visitors learn more about Cusco and discover places and experiences they can enjoy during their visit.

Scenarios

  • What are the most important attractions to visit in Cusco?
  • What are the traditional foods I can try when visiting Cusco?
  • What is the best time of the year to visit Cusco?

Color Schema

The website will use colors inspired by the landscape and cultural heritage of Cusco.

  • Dark Brown (#4B2E2A): Used for headings, navigation, and other main elements.
  • Gold (#D9A441): Used for accents, buttons, and decorative elements.
  • Cream (#F5F1E8): Used as the main background color.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Used for text on dark backgrounds.

Typography

  • Roboto: Used for headings and titles.
  • Arial: Used for body text, paragraphs, and lists.
